Questions

Gauge Case FAQ

What gauges does this fit?

Standard CAT diagnostic pressure gauges—each case is molded to fit snug with no rattle.

Will the magnets damage my equipment or mess with electronics?

No. They stick to steel surfaces—cab frames, ROPS posts, boom arms—without marking paint or metal. Just keep them away from sensitive electronics or magnetic-strip cards.

Can I pick my own colors?

Yes. Choose your top and bottom color for each case in the form at checkout—Blue, Red, Green, Yellow, or Black.

Does $197 get me all 5 cases?

Yes—one order is 5 color-coded cases, each with your choice of top and bottom color.

How long until my order ships?

Every set is made to order in Henderson, NV. Check the product page at checkout for current turnaround.

Will it hold up in extreme heat or cold?

TPU stays flexible in the cold instead of cracking, and holds its shape in heat better than standard 3D-print plastic—built for real jobsite temps, not a climate-controlled shop.

Does this work on equipment other than CAT?

Each case is molded specifically for standard CAT diagnostic pressure gauges. It's designed and tested for CAT gauges—fit on other brands isn't guaranteed.

Can I order just one case instead of the full set?

Yes — individual cases are $45 each. The 5-case set at $197 works out cheaper per case and is the better value if you're color-coding pilot, pump, steering, and brake pressure all at once.

What's the difference between the top and bottom color?

Each case has two halves you can color independently. Most mechanics match the top to the pressure line and use the bottom as a second ID marker—set it up however makes sense on your board.

Will the magnets hold on painted or coated steel?

Yes. Neodymium magnets pull through paint and standard coatings on cab frames, ROPS posts, and boom arms without issue. Heavily textured or non-ferrous surfaces (aluminum, fiberglass) won't hold a magnet.

How do I clean the case?

Wipe it down with a shop rag and a mild solvent. TPU resists the chemicals you're already using around the shop.
